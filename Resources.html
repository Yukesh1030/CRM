What is STACKLY CRM?

STACKLY CRM is a comprehensive customer relationship management platform designed to help businesses manage leads, track sales pipelines, and automate communication all in one place.

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No, you do not need a credit card to start your 14-day free trial. You can explore all features risk-free.

Can I import data from my old CRM?

Yes, we offer a simple CSV import tool and direct integrations with popular CRMs to make migration seamless.
